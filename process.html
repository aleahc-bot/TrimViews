Step by step

From first conversation to final walkthrough.

Every Trim Views project follows the same clear, collaborative path — so the experience is as refined as the finished work.

01

Consultation

We start with a conversation about your vision, your space, and how you live in it — on site or over a call — to understand exactly what you want to achieve.

02

Design

We translate your ideas into a clear design and layout, collaborating with your designer or builder so every detail fits the bigger picture.

03

Proposal

You receive a detailed, itemized proposal — scope, materials, and timeline — so you know exactly what's included before anything begins.

04

Material Selection

We help you choose the woods, profiles, and finishes that match your home and taste, sourcing premium materials built to last.

05

Fabrication

Your pieces are custom-built to precise measurements in the shop and on site, with the joinery and detailing that set fine carpentry apart.

06

Installation

Our team installs with care and precision, protecting your home throughout and holding every cut, miter, and joint to an exacting standard.

07

Final Walkthrough

We walk the finished project with you, reviewing every detail together and making sure it's flawless before we call it complete.
